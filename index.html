The Azov Legacy

I. Born in Fire (2014 - 2022)

The Azov Brigade began as a volunteer unit, formed by those who refused to let their country fall. For 8 years, they held the line in Donbas, evolving into one of the most professional and disciplined units in the Ukrainian Armed Forces. They became the shield that the enemy could never break.

II. The Fortress of Mariupol

In 2022, the world watched Azov defend Mariupol for 86 days of total encirclement. Against a force 10 times their size, they turned the Azovstal steel plant into an impregnable fortress. They didn't just fight; they showed the world that freedom is worth the ultimate price. The 1st Mechanized Battalion carries this spirit into every battle today.

III. Today: The Mechanized Spearhead

Today, the 1st Mechanized Battalion operates as an elite mobile force. We specialize in high-intensity urban warfare and rapid mechanized assaults. Every vehicle we operate and every drone we fly is a tool used to reclaim sovereign territory and protect democratic values on the global frontline.
